# WaterBuddy V1

**Branch:** `v1`, built on `hero-lab` (Hero Lab checkpoint `ea1d5bf` and QA fixes `e910284`).

**What V1 is:** the existing app (Phases 0–8: tracker, Journal, postcards, reminders, every age, privacy, PWA) with the Hero Lab's Ripple and forest spring built into it, replacing Pip. One app, three destinations (Today, Atlas, Journal), plus You (settings).

## What's in it

### Today
- **Ripple's spring as the stage.** The water rises with the day's total. Every drink pours in from the bamboo spout, and Ripple reacts: happy, curious, and a single celebration at the goal. Tap Ripple, the water, a pad or the spout for a response. A swipe over the spring scrolls the screen rather than turning it.
- **Logging:**
  - quick-adds of **+150, +250 and +500 ml** by default, all editable in You, plus a 1 L bottle;
  - a **custom amount** (Other);
  - each drink can be edited or deleted from the list.
- **Progress:** total, **percentage**, the **remaining amount** ("1,100 ml to go"), and a progress bar.
- **Undo** on every drink's toast removes exactly that drink. An **accidental double tap logs once**; deliberate repeat taps all count.
- **Milestones:** stamps at 25/50/75/100%.
- **Celebration:** once a day. Undoing the goal drink and logging it again doesn't celebrate twice. Past the goal, drinks are logged accurately with no reward.
- **Kept from before:** the sip guard ("Sip, don't rush", over a litre in an hour) and last call (two hours before sleep, Today stops counting down).
- **Tip of the day:** 14 gentle, non-medical field notes (food counts too, more isn't better, follow a doctor's limit).
- **Daily rollover:** at midnight while open, and on return to the app. Records are kept on the device (IndexedDB) and survive reloads.

### Atlas: Ripple's spring
- **The Hero Lab experience, full screen:**
  - drag to look around; pinch, scroll or trackpad to zoom, within natural limits;
  - tap Ripple (reacts by state), the water (tap for a ring, press and hold for a plunge), lily pads (dip, turn, drift) and the bamboo spout (a trickle that never logs);
  - water effects, reflections and environmental life;
  - the "Explore the spring" guide.
- **Daily quest:** find the four things that respond. Progress shows as 0–4, and completing it is announced.
- **Pour from the spring:** logs exactly like Today, with the same guards and Undo.
- **Without a graphics chip** (software rendering), or with 3D off, the spring shows a still picture with an explanation and a "Show it anyway" option. Logging is never affected.

### Journal
- **This week's tide marks** (as before).
- **Progress:**
  - this week and the last 30 days: days at goal, average per day (counted over every day since starting), days logged and best day;
  - a month strip (full dot: goal met; half dot: some water).
- **The spring:** today's quest progress and quests completed this week.
- **Kept from before:** today's field task (the daily sidequest: 14 tasks), stickers, postcards (Route I, one card the morning after each goal day), a line a day (reflections), and the full drink history with each day against that day's goal.

### Onboarding and personalisation
- **Steps:** welcome (Ripple), age band (4–8, 9–13, 14–17, 18–64, 65+), goal, cups and units.
- **Goals:** suggested goals come with their reason and source, and are labelled **"A starting point, not a prescription"** (set your own, or follow a doctor). The goal guard asks for confirmation outside the usual range.
- **Under 13:** a grown-up check guards goal and data changes. No accounts, profiles, social features or links out for minors; under 18, reminders stay on the device (no server). This hasn't had a legal review; see "Remaining limitations".

### You (settings and utilities)
- **Goal:** daily goal and age band.
- **Amounts:** units (ml, cups, fl oz), your own cup size, your vessels (custom cup and bottle sizes), and a **cups ⇄ ml ⇄ fl oz converter**.
- **Display:** text size, high contrast, and motion (follow the phone, full, less).
- **Ripple and the spring:** 3D on or off, and sounds (off by default).
- **Your day:** wake-up and sleep times, which set the quiet hours (from last call until you wake).
- **Reminders:** one gentle daily web-push reminder, only between waking and last call. It never comes after the goal, and is adults only. It needs notification permission, the service worker and the reminder server. On iPhone, the app must first be added to the Home Screen.
- **Install, privacy, export data, delete everything.**

### PWA and offline
- **Installable:** manifest, icons and the service worker.
- **Offline after the first visit:** the app, Ripple's compressed model (1 MB) and the spring's still picture are cached. The 2.3 MB original model isn't cached; it's only a fallback.
- **Development tools:** DEV tools, test hooks and tester overrides show only in development or with `?dev`. The Hero Lab page is still at `/lab/` for QA.

## Remaining limitations

- **Real devices:** not tested yet. The manual procedure is `docs/hero-lab-device-testing.md`, and it applies to Today and Atlas too. Check Safari's own gestures, real GPUs, heat, VoiceOver and TalkBack, and the iPhone silent switch.
- **Reminders:**
  - The server must allow the new web address (`WB_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` on Render).
  - The scheduler token issue from the earlier deploy session is unresolved.
  - Render's free tier sleeps, so the first request can take about 40 s.
  - Background delivery to real phones is unverified.
- **One reminder a day:** that's the reminder system's design. More frequent reminders would need server changes.
- **Children and teens:** the age-band protections are in place, but nothing claims regulatory compliance. A legal and health review is still needed (D-011).
- **Older devices:** phones without a graphics chip get the still spring, not the live one. Mid-range phones are unmeasured.
- **Old code still in the repo:** Pip, tidepool and Waterverse files remain in `src/three` and `src/components`, unused by the app. They can be deleted in a cleanup.
