# Safety rules

These rules beat any engagement idea. If a feature conflicts with one, the feature changes.

## Hydration
1. **Never reward drinking past the goal.** Logs over the goal are recorded but earn nothing: no progress, no postcard, no stamp.
2. **Reminders stop at the goal**, and never increase in frequency. Repeatedly dismissed reminders get quieter.
3. **Last call.** Two hours before the user's bedtime, Pip stops asking. If the goal isn't met: "We'll sail tomorrow." This also protects sleep.
4. **Sip, don't rush.** More than about 1 litre logged within an hour shows a calm note. A health reviewer confirms the threshold and wording before launch.
5. **Goal guard.** Goals outside a sensible range for the age band need a confirmation and show a note. Kid-mode goals are set by a parent.
6. **No diagnosis.** Never say or imply "you are dehydrated". Pip never looks sick, thirsty or sad because of the user's logs.
7. **Count fluids honestly.** Label totals as "fluids logged". India's NIN 2024 guideline describes about 2 litres of water including beverages for a healthy adult; needs are higher in heat and activity.
8. **Heat days** show a short sourced note; the user decides whether to raise today's goal. The app never raises it on its own.

## Motivation
9. **No streaks to lose.** Missed days never remove progress, never send places back into the fog, and are never mentioned.
10. **No purchases that change progress**, and no purchases at all in kid mode.

## Privacy and age
11. **Drink logs stay on the device** unless a person opts in to sync.
12. **No social features under 18.** India's DPDP framework treats everyone under 18 as a child.
13. **Every fact has a source** in `content/` and a reviewer before it ships.
